What I hold on to

Four knots that haven't slipped yet.

  • Listen first

    Most bugs begin as misunderstandings. I ask the people, and the designers, before I ask the compiler.

  • Nothing in the way

    Speed is not a phase at the end. Every kilobyte is a moment someone spends waiting.

  • Leave a light on

    Keyboard, screen reader, slow phone, tired eyes. Everyone finds the door.

  • Hold it together

    Shared components, clear patterns, one design language, so a whole team can speak without shouting.
